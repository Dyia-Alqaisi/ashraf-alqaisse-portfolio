02Expertise

Every window on the heart

Adult and pediatric echocardiography across all modalities.

2D Echo

Real-time imaging of cardiac structure, chambers and wall motion.

Doppler

Spectral Doppler measurement of blood-flow velocities and pressure gradients.

Color Flow

Color-flow mapping of blood-flow direction across valves and chambers.

Stress Echo

Wall-motion assessment of the heart at rest and under stress.

TEE

Transesophageal echocardiography for close, high-resolution views.

Pediatric Echo

Independent pediatric studies, with referral to pediatric cardiology.
